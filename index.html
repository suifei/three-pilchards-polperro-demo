On the quay

Harbour inn, historic setting

The oldest pub in Polperro sits on Quay Road, in a 16th-century Grade 2 listed building. The name comes from the pilchard catch that once made this harbour a busy little port.

Everyone is welcome, including dogs. Local ales, ciders and lagers, local spirits and a wide wine list sit behind the bar. Food is fresh and local; staff keep an eye on the room.

Rooftop

Harbour-view beer garden

The garden is to the rear of the restaurant: first level up 31 steps, second level another 17. The view is worth it. It is not for wheelchairs, prams or highchairs — elevation, narrowness, space and safety.

31steps to the first level
17more to the second

Weather

Parasols for a shower. Sitting in the garden is at your own risk. If the weather turns they may not be able to seat you inside.

Highchairs

Highchairs inside the pub and restaurant only — none in the garden.

Inside

A small 10cm step at the front door. The bar and restaurant are reasonably level. Pushchairs folded, please, so walkways stay clear. Arrangements can be made for wheelchair users who want to eat and drink inside.

Toilets

To the rear, up 4 steps through the restaurant, or by the back gate on a short slope. No disabled toilet — age of the building. Ladies, urinal and unisex. Public toilets about 50 metres down, 50p, Easter to early November, with baby change.
